قصتنا · Our Story

أهلاً بيك

September Cafe is a slow, deliberate kind of coffee house — the kind where the espresso is pulled with care and the seating invites you to stay a while. Our bar runs on hand-poured V60s and Chemex alongside classic espresso drinks, matcha, iced teas and mojitos, with a daily case of cakes made in-house.

As the wall behind our bar puts it: "Coffee & friends make the perfect blend." Come pull up a chair.
